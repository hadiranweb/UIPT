# ONSOUR Investor Site — Design Direction

## Approach 1
**Theme Name:** Living Infrastructure  
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark editorial systems aesthetic that treats the runtime as a living organism: precise typography, scientific diagrams, and soft energy fields replace noisy cyberpunk tropes. The result should feel credible to investors and legible to engineers.  
**Probability:** 0.06

## Approach 2
**Theme Name:** Civic Instrument  
**Very Brief Intro:** A light, institutional interface inspired by observatories and public research labs, using paper-like surfaces, measured typography, and annotated diagrams. It would make ONSOUR feel open, trusted, and policy-ready.  
**Probability:** 0.03

## Approach 3
**Theme Name:** Quiet Machine  
**Very Brief Intro:** A monochrome, gallery-like product narrative with restrained color, large editorial headlines, and sparse interaction. It would position ONSOUR as an elegant infrastructure layer rather than a conventional software product.  
**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen Direction: Living Infrastructure

### Design Movement
Contemporary systems editorialism: a hybrid of scientific publishing, high-end product design, and observatory instrumentation.

### Core Principles
1. **Pressure becomes visible.** Telemetry, load, latency, and dispersion should be expressed as visual energy rather than hidden in feature lists.
2. **Precision over spectacle.** Use a narrow palette, sharp typographic hierarchy, and carefully paced motion; never let the visual system feel louder than the idea.
3. **The organism is the metaphor.** Soft orbital loops, nodes, and fields recur throughout the site to suggest feedback, homeostasis, and memory.
4. **Every claim earns a mechanism.** Pair investor-facing language with an adjacent implementation detail so the pitch remains credible.

### Color Philosophy
Ink blue anchors the experience as the color of a deep instrument room: serious, calm, and continuous. Cyan indicates flow, telemetry, and the live system. Warm amber indicates pressure, attention, and the moment where governance intervenes. Bone and mist are reserved for legibility and section changes. The signature brand color is **ONSOUR Flux Cyan**: `#74F0E4`.

### Layout Paradigm
Use an asymmetrical editorial rail instead of a centered marketing grid. The hero has a narrow left copy column and a large right visual field. Sections alternate between left-anchored narrative and right-anchored instrumentation. Full-bleed imagery, thin rules, and oversized numeric moments create pacing without relying on repetitive cards.

### Signature Elements
- A compact orbital-loop symbol used as the brand mark, favicon, and section punctuation.
- Fine cyan/amber telemetry lines that trace through diagrams and respond to scroll or slider input.
- “Field notes” labels in a mono face, showing the implementation beneath every strategic promise.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like instrument calibration: deliberate, reversible, and readable. Sliders expose the dynamic epsilon model; the homeostasis loop highlights one phase at a time; buttons move the visitor to the next narrative beat rather than opening unrelated UI.

### Animation
Use low-amplitude orbital drift, line-draw reveals, and opacity/transform-only entrances. Motion should feel like a field stabilizing, not a dashboard flashing. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; in reduced mode, preserve hierarchy and state without movement.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for headlines and large numerals, with **IBM Plex Mono** for labels, formula annotations, and implementation notes. Headlines should be compact, slightly tight, and mostly sentence case. Labels use uppercase with generous letter spacing. Body copy stays at a calm 17–19px with an 1.6 line-height.

### Brand Essence
ONSOUR is a self-regulating runtime for resilient autonomous systems, built for teams that need adaptive stability without surrendering auditability.  
**Personality:** lucid, resilient, exacting.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are declarative and human: “Systems that know when to hold.” CTAs are specific and low-hype: “Explore the control loop.” Microcopy names the mechanism: “Raw telemetry enters. Authoritative state leaves.” Avoid generic filler such as “Welcome” or “Get started today.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark should be set as a custom typographic treatment paired with the orbital-loop mark. The symbol is a compact loop around a central node, designed to remain recognizable at 16px and to carry the identity when the full wordmark is absent.

### Signature Brand Color
**ONSOUR Flux Cyan — `#74F0E4`**. It is the visual signal of a system in balance: bright enough to guide the eye, restrained enough to stay credible against ink blue.

## Implementation Reminder
All page, component, and stylesheet files must preserve the Living Infrastructure direction: dark observatory atmosphere, asymmetric editorial composition, Space Grotesk + IBM Plex Mono, Flux Cyan and amber as restrained signals, and motion that communicates stabilization rather than spectacle.
